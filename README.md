# Digital Lab

**A personal space for technology, experimentation and continuous learning.**

Welcome to my Digital Lab: a public repository where I explore digital technologies, experiment with different tools and document my learning journey.

## Areas of Exploration

- **Cloud & Infrastructure** – AWS, Linux, networking, servers and security.
- **Programming & Automation** – Python, scripting, software development and automation.
- **Systems & Open Source** – Operating systems, open-source software and alternative platforms.
- **Hardware & Technology** – Computer hardware, digital systems and emerging technologies.
- **Creative Experiments** – Personal projects, unconventional ideas and technological experiments.

## Philosophy

This repository is a work in progress.

Not everything here needs to be a finished product. Some projects will be experiments, others will be practical exercises or technical notes.

The goal is to learn by building, testing, documenting and exploring.

**Explore. Experiment. Build. Repeat.**
